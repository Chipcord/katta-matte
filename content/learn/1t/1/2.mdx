## The Quadratic Formula

The quadratic formula is:

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

You can also write inline mathematics such as $x^2 + 2x + 1$.


## Pythagorean Theorem

For a right triangle:

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

Therefore:

$$
c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}
$$
